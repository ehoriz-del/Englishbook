# Chapter 38 — False Dawn

## Story Summary

The girls at Patty’s Place are preparing to leave. Miss Patty and Miss Maria are returning from their travels, so Anne, Phil, Priscilla, Stella, and Aunt Jamesina must give up the house that has been their home during their Redmond years.

Anne is happy that she will soon return to Avonlea, but she is also deeply sad to leave Patty’s Place. Her blue room has held years of study, laughter, friendship, disappointment, and hope.

Phil feels the same way. She is going to marry Jonas in June and expects to be very happy, yet she wishes for a moment that their carefree Redmond life could continue.

Aunt Jamesina plans to take Rusty, Joseph, and the Sarah-cat with her rather than separate them. Anne agrees that Rusty would not fit well at Green Gables, especially because Marilla dislikes cats and Davy would probably tease him.

Anne also tells Phil that she has been offered the position of principal at Summerside High School. She has not decided whether to accept it.

Phil assumes that Anne is waiting for Roy Gardner to propose before making plans. Everyone at Patty’s Place expects Roy’s proposal, and Anne herself believes she will accept him. For two years she has thought of him as the romantic man she wanted.

Still, Anne admits privately that love with Roy does not feel exactly as she once imagined love would feel. Something is missing, but she tells herself that real life is rarely exactly like imagination.

That evening Roy comes to Patty’s Place and asks Anne to walk with him in the park. Everyone understands why he has come.

Roy takes Anne to the little pavilion by the harbor where they first met during the rainstorm. Anne thinks the place is a perfect choice. His proposal is beautifully expressed and completely sincere.

Anne expects herself to be happy. She believes she should feel the excitement she has imagined for years.

Instead, when Roy asks her to marry him, Anne suddenly realizes that she cannot say yes.

At first she almost tries to give the expected answer. Then she becomes frightened by what she is about to do. She pulls her hand away and tells Roy she cannot marry him.

Roy is shocked. He asks why, and Anne says she does not care enough for him.

Roy believes she has encouraged him for two years without meaning what she seemed to mean. Anne desperately tries to explain that she truly thought she loved him. Only at the moment of his proposal did she understand that she did not.

Roy tells her bitterly that she has ruined his life. After a few moments he asks whether there is any hope that she may change her mind. Anne cannot give him any.

Roy says goodbye and leaves.

Anne remains alone in the pavilion for some time. She is ashamed and miserable because she knows Roy has been sincere and she has hurt him. At the same time, underneath the pain, she also feels unexpectedly free.

When Anne returns to Patty’s Place, Phil is waiting in her room. Anne immediately tells her that Roy proposed and she refused him.

Phil is astonished and angry. She points out that Anne has allowed Roy and everyone around them to believe for two years that she would eventually marry him.

Anne insists that she was not deliberately flirting. She really believed Roy was the man she loved.

Phil asks what more Anne could want. Roy is handsome, intelligent, wealthy, kind, and devoted to her.

Anne says that those qualities are not enough. She finally understands that Roy does not truly belong in her life. She had been attracted to his handsome appearance and romantic manner because he matched the dark-eyed ideal she had imagined since she was young.

Phil’s anger softens. She admits that real life is much more complicated than stories make it seem.

Anne cries and says she hopes no one will ever ask her to marry again. At that moment she feels that the future she had expected has disappeared, but she also knows she could not have honestly accepted Roy.

## Conversation 1 — Before Roy Arrives

Phil: You still haven’t decided about the Summerside job?
Anne: Not yet.
Phil: Are you **holding off** until Roy talks to you?
Anne: Maybe.
Phil: Everyone thinks he’s going to propose soon.
Anne: I know.
Phil: And if he does?
Anne: I’ve always assumed I’d **say yes**.
Phil: Then I guess tonight may **settle things**.

### Phrasal verbs & expressions

- hold off — delay doing something or making a decision. I’m holding off until I have more information.
- say yes — accept an offer or proposal. She said yes to the job.
- settle things — make a situation final or clear. One conversation may settle things.
- assume something — believe something is true without being completely certain. I assumed he would come.

## Conversation 2 — After the Proposal

Phil: You refused Roy?
Anne: Yes.
Phil: But I thought you loved him.
Anne: So did I, until the moment I had to **go through with** it.
Phil: What changed?
Anne: I suddenly knew I couldn’t **build a life with** him.
Phil: You must have shocked him.
Anne: I did. I feel terrible for **letting him down**.
Phil: Real feelings can be complicated.
Anne: I wish I had understood mine sooner.

### Phrasal verbs & expressions

- go through with — complete something you had planned or intended to do. She realized she could not go through with the wedding.
- build a life with — create a shared future with someone. I want to build a life with someone I truly love.
- let someone down — disappoint someone. I hated letting him down.
- figure out — understand something after thinking about it. I wish I had figured out my feelings earlier.
