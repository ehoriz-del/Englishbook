# Chapter 29 — An Epoch in Anne's Life

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Diana brings exciting news: Aunt Josephine has invited both girls to Charlottetown for an exhibition. Anne is thrilled to visit the city and stay in an elegant home. At first, the expensive rooms and city life seem like things Anne has always dreamed about.

But she discovers that luxury is less magical in reality than in her imagination. The exhibition is exciting, with displays, crowds, races, and many new sights. Aunt Josephine also takes the girls out for a late treat.

Anne enjoys the adventure, but she begins to miss Green Gables. Her own simple room and familiar home feel more comfortable than a grand house. When she returns, Anne tells Marilla that growing up changes the things a person wants.

Some dreams are more beautiful before they become real.

## Conversation 1 — The Invitation

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Diana: Aunt Josephine wants us to come up to Charlottetown.
Anne: I can hardly take it in!
Diana: We should get ready for the trip.

### Phrasal Verbs

- come up to — travel to a place.
- take in — fully understand surprising information.
- get ready for — prepare for.

## Conversation 2 — Coming Home

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Marilla: Did the city live up to your expectations?
Anne: Not completely. I may have grown out of some dreams.
Marilla: Green Gables has won you over.

### Useful Expressions

- live up to — be as good as expected.
- grow out of — stop wanting something as you mature.
- win someone over — cause someone to love or support something.
