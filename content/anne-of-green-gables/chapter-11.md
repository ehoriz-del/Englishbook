# Chapter 11 — Anne's Impressions of Sunday School

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Marilla makes Anne plain new dresses and sends her to church and Sunday school. Anne is grateful to have proper clothes, but she secretly wishes for fashionable puffed sleeves. On the way, Anne enjoys the countryside and decorates her hat with wildflowers.

She thinks it looks beautiful. At church, however, Anne is disappointed. She had imagined something inspiring, but she finds the sermon long and difficult. She also feels that Sunday school involves too much memorizing and not enough real thinking.

Anne later tells Marilla exactly what she thought. Her honesty worries Marilla, but it also shows that Anne wants things to have real meaning. Anne is learning the customs of Avonlea, but she is not giving up the imagination and curiosity that make her different.

## Conversation 1 — Getting Ready

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Marilla: Are you ready to head off to church?
Anne: Yes, although my dress doesn't exactly stand out.
Marilla: You're not going there to show off.

### Phrasal Verbs

- head off — leave for a place.
- stand out — be noticeably different.
- show off — try to attract attention.

## Conversation 2 — After Church

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Marilla: How did Sunday school go?
Anne: I tried to pay attention, but my mind wandered.
Marilla: You'll get used to it.

### Useful Expressions

- go — happen or progress. How did the interview go?
- pay attention — listen carefully.
- get used to — become comfortable with something.
