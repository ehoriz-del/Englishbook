# Chapter 17 — A New Interest in Life

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Diana secretly visits Anne to say that her mother still refuses to let them spend time together. The girls have an emotional goodbye. Anne is miserable, but she decides to return to school because at least she can see Diana there. Her classmates welcome her back warmly. School gives Anne a new purpose.

She becomes especially interested in competing with Gilbert academically. Both are intelligent and ambitious, and the rivalry pushes Anne to work harder. Anne discovers that disappointment does not have to stop her life.

She still misses Diana outside school, but learning and competition give her new motivation.

## Conversation 1 — Going Back

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: I've decided to go back to school.
Marilla: Good.
Anne: I need something to take my mind off Diana.
Marilla: And don't fall behind in your lessons.

### Phrasal Verbs

- go back to — return to a place or activity.
- take your mind off — stop thinking about something upsetting.
- fall behind — fail to stay at the same level.

## Conversation 2 — Competition

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Diana: You and Gilbert always try to outdo each other.
Anne: He makes me work harder.
Diana: Maybe someday you'll make up with him.

### Useful Expressions

- outdo — do better than someone else.
- make up with — become friendly again.
