# Chapter 18 — Anne to the Rescue

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

One winter evening, Diana arrives at Green Gables in a panic. Her little sister Minnie May is seriously ill with croup, and the adults who normally care for her are away. Anne immediately goes to the Barry home.

Because she once cared for Mrs. Hammond's children, she recognizes the illness and knows what to do. Anne stays calm and cares for Minnie May until the doctor arrives. The doctor later says that Anne's quick action probably saved the child's life.

Mrs. Barry is deeply grateful. She realizes she judged Anne too harshly after the wine accident and allows Anne and Diana to be friends again. Anne's difficult childhood has unexpectedly given her useful knowledge. Avonlea begins to see her not simply as a strange orphan who makes mistakes, but as a capable and courageous girl.

## Conversation 1 — An Emergency

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Diana: Can you come over right now?
Anne: Yes. We need to stay calm and look after Minnie May.
Diana: Thank you.

### Phrasal Verbs

- come over — come to someone's home.
- stay calm — remain calm in a difficult situation.
- look after — take care of.

## Conversation 2 — Mrs. Barry Changes Her Mind

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Mrs. Barry: You've proved yourself.
Anne: Can Diana and I get together again?
Mrs. Barry: Yes. I'm glad everything worked out.

### Useful Expressions

- prove yourself — show that you are capable or trustworthy.
- get together — meet and spend time together.
- work out — end successfully.
