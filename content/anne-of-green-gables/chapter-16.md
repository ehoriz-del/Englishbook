# Chapter 16 — Diana Is Invited to Tea with Tragic Results

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Marilla leaves Anne in charge of Green Gables for a day and allows her to invite Diana to tea. Anne is delighted and wants everything to feel grown-up and elegant. Anne believes she is serving raspberry cordial.

Diana likes it and drinks several glasses. Soon she becomes dizzy and unwell. The drink was actually currant wine. Anne had taken the wrong bottle without realizing it.

Mrs. Barry is furious. Although Anne explains that it was an innocent mistake, Mrs. Barry forbids Diana from spending time with her. Anne is heartbroken. Losing Diana feels like a terrible tragedy. Marilla knows Anne is innocent and defends her, but Mrs. Barry refuses to change her mind.

## Conversation 1 — Something Is Wrong

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: Maybe you should lie down.
Diana: I think I need to go home.
Anne: I'll walk you back.

### Phrasal Verbs

- lie down — put your body in a resting position.
- go home — return home.
- walk someone back — walk with someone to where they came from.

## Conversation 2 — Mrs. Barry Is Angry

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: I mixed up the bottles.
Mrs. Barry: Diana won't be coming over anymore.
Anne: Please don't keep us apart.

### Useful Expressions

- mix up — confuse one thing with another.
- come over — visit someone's home.
- keep apart — prevent people from being together.
