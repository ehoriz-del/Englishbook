# Chapter 1 — Mrs. Rachel Lynde Is Surprised

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

It is a quiet afternoon in the small village of Avonlea on Prince Edward Island. Mrs. Rachel Lynde is sitting by her window. She likes to know what is happening in the neighborhood and usually notices anything unusual.

Then she sees Matthew Cuthbert driving past her house. This surprises her. Matthew is a very quiet and shy man. He does not often go out, and today he is wearing his best clothes.

Mrs. Rachel immediately begins to wonder where he is going. She decides to visit Green Gables, where Matthew lives with his sister, Marilla. When Mrs. Rachel arrives, Marilla gives her some surprising news: she and Matthew have decided to adopt an orphan boy.

Matthew is getting older, and the farm is becoming difficult for him to manage alone. They hope that a boy of about ten or eleven can live with them, go to school, and also help Matthew with the farm work. Mrs. Rachel is shocked. She worries that bringing a child they know nothing about into their home could cause trouble.

She tells Marilla several frightening stories she has heard about adopted children. Marilla understands her concerns, but she and Matthew have already made the decision. Matthew is now on his way to the train station to pick up the boy.

Mrs. Rachel leaves Green Gables still thinking about the surprising news. But neither woman knows that Matthew is not going to find the boy they are expecting.

## Conversation 1 — What’s Going On?

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*
Neighbor: Rachel, you look surprised. What’s going on?
Rachel: You won’t believe it. Matthew and Marilla have decided to take in an orphan.
Neighbor: Really? How did that come about?
Rachel: They need someone to help Matthew out around the farm.
Neighbor: That’s a big decision.
Rachel: Exactly. I don’t know how it’s going to work out.

### Phrasal Verbs

- take in — allow someone to live in your home and care for them. They decided to take in an orphan.
- come about — happen or develop. How did this situation come about?
- help someone out — help someone with a problem or task. Can you help me out this weekend?
- work out — develop successfully. I hope everything works out.

## Conversation 2 — Matthew’s Trip

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*
Rachel: Where has Matthew gone?
Marilla: He’s gone to the station to pick up the boy.
Rachel: You really went through with it?
Marilla: Yes. We’ve been thinking about it for months.
Rachel: You’re certainly taking on a lot of responsibility.
Marilla: Matthew can’t keep up with all the farm work by himself anymore.

### Useful Expressions

- pick someone up — go somewhere and bring someone back. I’ll pick you up at the airport.
- go through with — do something you had planned, especially something difficult.
- take on — accept responsibility or work.
- keep up with — continue at the necessary speed or level.
