# Chapter 34 — A Queen's Girl

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Anne leaves Green Gables to attend Queen's Academy in Charlottetown. The move is exciting but difficult because she has never lived away from the home she loves. At Queen's, Anne meets talented students and begins an intense academic year.

She works hard toward a teaching license. Gilbert is also there. Their rivalry continues, but Anne no longer feels the same anger. She respects him and sometimes wishes they could be friends.

Anne makes new friends while staying close to Avonlea through letters and visits. She especially misses Marilla, Matthew, Diana, and Green Gables. Her ambitions grow. Anne decides to compete for the Avery Scholarship, which could allow her to continue at Redmond College.

## Conversation 1 — Leaving Home

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Diana: You'll settle in once classes begin.

### Phrasal Verbs

- move away — leave your home area to live elsewhere.
- leave behind — go away from people or places.
- settle in — become comfortable in a new place.

## Conversation 2 — New Goals

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: I'm going for the Avery Scholarship.
Friend: If you keep up your grades, you have a chance.
Anne: Then I'm going to go for it.

### Useful Expressions

- go for — try to achieve something.
- keep up — maintain a standard.
- go for it — try enthusiastically.
