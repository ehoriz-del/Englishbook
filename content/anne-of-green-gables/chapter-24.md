# Chapter 24 — Miss Stacy and Her Pupils Get Up a Concert

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Miss Stacy becomes Avonlea's new teacher, and Anne quickly admires her. Miss Stacy uses lively methods, encourages exercise, and makes lessons more interesting. Some adults are suspicious of her modern ideas, but the students respond enthusiastically.

Miss Stacy decides that the school should organize a Christmas concert to raise money for a Canadian flag. Anne is thrilled and receives several parts, including recitations. Marilla thinks the concert creates unnecessary excitement.

Matthew, however, quietly enjoys Anne's enthusiasm. Miss Stacy also recognizes Anne's intelligence and encourages her. School is beginning to shape Anne's ambitions for the future.

### Phrasal Verbs

- put on — organize and perform a show.
- raise money for — collect money for a purpose.
- throw yourself into — do something with great energy.
