# Chapter 35 — The Winter at Queen's

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Winter at Queen's passes quickly because Anne is busy with classes and studying. She becomes more comfortable in Charlottetown and makes new friends. Her competition with Gilbert remains strong but is now respectful rather than hostile.

Anne recognizes that Gilbert is a worthy opponent and that competing with him has helped her improve. Anne sometimes wishes they were friends, but her old pride still stops her from making the first move. She continues preparing for the final examinations and the Avery Scholarship.

Her goals are demanding, but she has learned to work steadily. Anne still values imagination, beauty, and friendship, but ambition has become another important part of her personality. She also understands more clearly that doing her best matters even if she does not always win.

## Conversation 1 — Keeping Up

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Friend: How are you keeping up with the work?
Anne: I've got into a good routine.
Friend: Still trying to beat out Gilbert?
Anne: I want to do well, but winning isn't everything.

### Phrasal Verbs

- keep up with — stay at the same pace.
- get into — develop a habit or routine.
- beat out — defeat someone in a competition.

## Conversation 2 — A Changing Rivalry

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Friend: Why don't you two make peace?
Anne: Sometimes I want to.
Friend: What holds you back?
Anne: Pride. I should probably let go of it.

### Useful Expressions

- make peace — end a conflict.
- hold back — prevent someone from acting.
- let go of — stop holding onto an emotion.
