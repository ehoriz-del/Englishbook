# Chapter 36 — The Glory and the Dream

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

The final examination results are announced. Gilbert wins the Gold Medal, while Anne wins the Avery Scholarship. The scholarship means Anne can attend Redmond College.

She is thrilled. Years of study have opened a future she once could hardly imagine. Anne returns to Green Gables. Matthew tells her how proud he is and reminds her that he always preferred having her, even though he and Marilla originally asked for a boy. Anne is deeply happy: she has a loving home, academic success, and a promising future.

At the same time, there are signs of trouble. Matthew has heart problems, and Marilla's eyesight is becoming worse. Anne does not yet understand how quickly her plans may change.

### Useful Expressions

- turn out — develop in a particular way.
- take someone in — give someone a home.

- look ahead to — think about or plan for something in the future. She was already looking ahead to the next stage of her life.
