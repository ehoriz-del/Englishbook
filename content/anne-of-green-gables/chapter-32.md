# Chapter 32 — The Pass List Is Out

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Anne and the other Queen's candidates finish their time at Avonlea school. Saying goodbye to Miss Stacy and their old school life is emotional. Anne goes to Charlottetown to take the entrance examinations.

She is nervous but follows Miss Stacy's advice not to study desperately at the last minute. Afterward, Anne returns to Green Gables and waits for the results. The waiting feels almost worse than the exams.

Finally, the newspaper publishes the pass list. All the Avonlea students have passed. Even more exciting, Anne and Gilbert have tied for first place on Prince Edward Island. Matthew, Marilla, Diana, and Mrs. Rachel are extremely proud.

Anne has earned a place at Queen's Academy and proved what talent and hard work can accomplish.

## Conversation 1 — Waiting

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Diana: Have the results come out?
Anne: Not yet. The waiting is wearing me down.
Diana: I'm sure you got through.

### Phrasal Verbs

- come out — be published or become available.
- wear someone down — gradually make someone tired or worried.
- get through — successfully pass or complete something.

## Conversation 2 — Good News

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Diana: You and Gilbert came out on top!
Anne: We tied for first?
Diana: All that work paid off.

### Useful Expressions

- come out on top — finish in the best position.
- pay off — produce a good result after effort.
