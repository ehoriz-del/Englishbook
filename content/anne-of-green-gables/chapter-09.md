# Chapter 9 — Mrs. Rachel Lynde Is Properly Horrified

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Two weeks after Anne's arrival, Mrs. Rachel Lynde visits Green Gables to see her. Marilla admits that she has already grown fond of Anne and that the house feels different with her there. When Anne comes inside, Mrs. Rachel looks at her closely and comments that she is thin and not pretty.

Worst of all, she makes fun of Anne's red hair. Anne's red hair is one of her greatest insecurities. She becomes furious and shouts at Mrs. Rachel.

Marilla is horrified. After Mrs. Rachel leaves, Marilla tells Anne that insulting a visitor is unacceptable even if the visitor was rude first. Anne refuses to apologize because she believes she was defending herself. Marilla sends her to her room until she is willing to say sorry.

Matthew quietly sympathizes with Anne, but Marilla insists that Anne must learn self-control.

## Conversation 1 — Losing Your Temper

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Marilla: You can't blow up at people like that.
Anne: But she picked on my hair!
Marilla: You still went too far.
Anne: I couldn't hold back.

### Phrasal Verbs

- blow up at — suddenly become angry with someone.
- pick on — criticize or treat someone unfairly.
- hold back — control an emotion or action.

## Conversation 2 — The Apology

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Matthew: Maybe you should get it over with and apologize.
Anne: Marilla won't give in, will she?
Matthew: Probably not. Then you can make up with Mrs. Lynde.

### Useful Expressions

- get it over with — finish something unpleasant.
- give in — stop resisting.
- make up with — become friendly again after an argument.
