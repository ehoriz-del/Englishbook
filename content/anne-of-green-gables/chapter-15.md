# Chapter 15 — A Tempest in the School Teapot

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Anne begins attending Avonlea school and enjoys studying with Diana and the other girls. Then Gilbert Blythe returns. He is confident and popular. When Anne ignores him, Gilbert pulls her braid and calls her "Carrots," teasing her about her red hair.

Anne is furious and breaks her slate over his head. Mr. Phillips punishes her and later makes her sit beside Gilbert, making her humiliation worse. Gilbert apologizes, but Anne refuses to forgive him.

She decides he will be her enemy. Anne becomes so upset that she refuses to return to school for a time. This event begins a long rivalry. Gilbert is interested in Anne, but Anne's pride and sensitivity turn their first meeting into a lasting conflict.

## Conversation 1 — At School

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Diana: Why did you go after Gilbert?
Anne: He made fun of my hair.
Diana: He was teasing.
Anne: I won't put up with that.

### Phrasal Verbs

- go after — confront or pursue someone.
- make fun of — laugh at someone unkindly.
- put up with — accept something unpleasant.

## Conversation 2 — Cooling Off

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Marilla: You need to cool off.
Anne: I'm not going back.
Marilla: In time, you may get over it.

### Useful Expressions

- cool off — become less angry.
- get over — recover emotionally from something.
