# Chapter 12 — A Solemn Vow and Promise

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Marilla arranges for Anne to meet Diana Barry. Anne is extremely excited because she has always dreamed of having a true best friend. Anne and Diana are left alone to get acquainted.

Anne quickly asks Diana whether she will become her "bosom friend." Diana agrees, though she is less dramatic about it. Outside, the girls make a serious promise to remain friends forever. Anne treats the moment like an important ceremony.

Diana happily joins her. Their friendship develops immediately. Diana is kind and practical, and she enjoys Anne's imaginative personality. Anne finally has someone her own age with whom she can share thoughts and adventures.

When Matthew later gives Anne chocolates, she wants to share half with Diana. Marilla is pleased by Anne's generous spirit.

## Conversation 1 — Meeting Diana

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: I hope we can get to know each other.
Diana: I'd like that.
Anne: Maybe we'll hit it off.
Diana: I think we already have.

### Phrasal Verbs

- get to know — gradually learn more about someone.
- hit it off — quickly become friendly.

## Conversation 2 — A Promise

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: We must never fall out.
Diana: And if we do, we'll talk it out.
Anne: Then we can always count on each other.

### Useful Expressions

- fall out — have an argument and stop being friendly.
- talk it out — discuss a problem until it is resolved.
- count on — depend on someone.
