# Chapter 4 — Morning at Green Gables

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

The next morning, Anne wakes up in the small bedroom at Green Gables. For a moment, she does not remember where she is. Then she looks outside. The morning is beautiful.

Anne sees trees, flowers, fields, and the countryside around Green Gables. She becomes so excited by the beauty outside that she almost forgets how unhappy she was the night before. At breakfast, Marilla notices that Anne is hardly eating.

Anne explains that she is too upset to have much of an appetite because she still believes she will be sent away. Marilla tells her that they have not made a final decision yet. Later, Marilla prepares to take Anne to Mrs. Spencer, the woman who arranged the adoption.

She wants to find out exactly how the mistake happened. Anne understands what the trip probably means. She expects that she will eventually be sent back to the orphanage.

Even so, Anne continues to notice the beauty around Green Gables. Marilla finds Anne’s constant talking unusual and sometimes tiring, but she is beginning to see that Anne is intelligent, sensitive, and full of life.

### Phrasal Verbs

- send someone back — make someone return to where they came from.
- cheer up — become happier or make someone happier.

## Conversation 2 — Finding Out What Happened

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Marilla: We’re going to Mrs. Spencer’s today.
Anne: Are you going to take me back?
Marilla: First, I want to find out how this happened.
Anne: Maybe everything will turn out all right.

### Useful Expressions

- take someone back — return someone to a previous place.
- find out — discover information.
- turn out — develop or end in a particular way.
