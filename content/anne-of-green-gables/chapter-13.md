# Chapter 13 — The Delights of Anticipation

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Anne learns that the Sunday school is planning a picnic. She has never attended a real picnic before, and the thought fills her with excitement. The most exciting detail is that ice cream will be served.

Anne has never tasted it and imagines how wonderful it will be. She tells Marilla that looking forward to something can be almost as enjoyable as the event itself. Marilla agrees to let Anne go and promises to prepare food.

Anne is so grateful that she hugs and kisses Marilla. Marilla is embarrassed but secretly pleased. Anne spends the following days thinking about the picnic and hoping that nothing will prevent her from attending.

An ordinary community event becomes something wonderful through Anne's ability to anticipate happiness.

## Conversation 1 — The Picnic

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: I can't stop looking forward to the picnic.
Marilla: Don't let it get in the way of your chores.
Anne: I'll catch up on everything.

### Phrasal Verbs

- look forward to — feel excited about a future event.
- get in the way of — interfere with something.
- catch up on — complete work you have not finished.

## Conversation 2 — Waiting

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Diana: What if it rains?
Anne: Don't bring that up!
Diana: All right. We'll hope for good weather.

### Useful Expressions

- bring up — start talking about a subject.
- hope for — want something good to happen.
