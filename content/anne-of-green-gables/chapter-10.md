# Chapter 10 — Anne's Apology

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Anne remains in her room because she does not want to apologize. Matthew secretly asks her to do it so the punishment can end. Anne finally agrees. At Mrs. Rachel's house, her apology becomes far more dramatic than Marilla expected.

Anne kneels down, admits that she behaved badly, and passionately asks for forgiveness. Mrs. Rachel is surprised but pleased. She accepts the apology and even tells Anne that her red hair may become a nicer color as she grows older. On the way home, Anne admits that apologizing was almost enjoyable because she could make it dramatic.

Marilla tries not to laugh. The conflict is repaired. Anne has learned that relationships sometimes require humility, while Marilla learns that Anne experiences even apologies with unusual intensity.

## Conversation 1 — Owning Up

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Matthew: Just own up to what you did.
Anne: I suppose I should get it over with.
Matthew: Then you two can start over.

### Phrasal Verbs

- own up to — admit that you did something wrong.
- get it over with — finish an unpleasant task.
- start over — begin again.

## Conversation 2 — Afterward

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Marilla: Mrs. Lynde seems to have come around.
Anne: I'm glad. Next time I'll try not to lash out.
Marilla: Good. Learn from what happened.

### Useful Expressions

- come around — change your opinion positively.
- lash out — suddenly speak or act angrily.
