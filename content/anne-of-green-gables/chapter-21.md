# Chapter 21 — A New Departure in Flavorings

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Avonlea goes through several changes. Mr. Phillips leaves the school, and a young minister, Mr. Allan, and his wife come to the community. Anne immediately admires Mrs. Allan because she is warm and encourages children to ask questions. Marilla invites the Allans to tea. Anne is allowed to bake a special layer cake and wants desperately to impress Mrs. Allan.

Unfortunately, Anne accidentally uses anodyne liniment instead of vanilla flavoring. The cake tastes terrible. Anne believes she has ruined the visit. Mrs. Allan treats the mistake kindly, which makes Anne admire her even more.

Soon Anne also learns that a new teacher, Miss Stacy, will come to Avonlea, bringing another important change.

## Conversation 1 — The Cake

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Marilla: What did you put in the cake?
Anne: I thought I picked up the vanilla.
Marilla: This is liniment!

### Phrasal Verbs

- put in — add something.
- pick up — take or choose something.

## Conversation 2 — Mrs. Allan's Reaction

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Mrs. Allan: Don't beat yourself up over a cake.
Anne: I wanted it to be perfect.
Mrs. Allan: Let's move on and enjoy the afternoon.

### Useful Expressions

- beat yourself up — criticize yourself too harshly.
- move on — stop focusing on a problem and continue.

- end up — finally be in a particular situation, often unexpectedly. We ended up eating dinner much later than planned.
