# Chapter 14 — Anne's Confession

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Just before the picnic, Marilla cannot find her amethyst brooch. Anne had admired it, so Marilla asks whether she touched it. Anne admits trying it on but says she put it back.

Marilla does not believe her and says Anne cannot attend the picnic until she confesses. Desperate to go, Anne invents a confession. She says she took the brooch outside and accidentally lost it in the water.

Then Marilla discovers the brooch caught on her own shawl. Anne had told the truth at first. Marilla realizes she pressured Anne into confessing to something she did not do. Anne explains that she invented the story because she thought a confession was the only way to get permission to attend the picnic.

Marilla apologizes and lets her go. Both learn a lesson: adults can make unfair assumptions, and Anne's imagination can create trouble when an invented story is presented as fact.

## Conversation 1 — The Missing Brooch

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Marilla: The brooch has gone missing.
Anne: I put it back.
Marilla: We need to figure this out.

### Phrasal Verbs

- go missing — become lost or disappear.
- figure out — understand or solve something.

## Conversation 2 — The Truth

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Marilla: The brooch turned up on my shawl.
Anne: Then you know I told the truth.
Marilla: I jumped to conclusions. I should have listened.

### Useful Expressions

- turn up — be found unexpectedly.
- jump to conclusions — decide too quickly without enough facts.

- miss out on — lose the chance to experience or do something. I don’t want to miss out on the picnic.
