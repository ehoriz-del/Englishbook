# Chapter 26 — The Story Club Is Formed

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Anne and several friends form a story club. They agree to write original stories and read them to one another. Anne loves the idea because storytelling has always been central to her imagination.

At first, the girls write very dramatic stories filled with romance and tragedy. Marilla thinks the club is another example of Anne wasting time, but Anne argues that writing can improve their skills. Over time, Anne becomes more thoughtful about writing.

She begins to understand the difference between simply inventing dramatic events and creating a good story. Her imagination, which once caused problems, is becoming a useful talent because writing gives her a place to direct it.

## Conversation 1 — Starting the Club

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: Let's set up a story club.
Diana: What would we do?
Anne: Come up with a story every week and read it out.

### Phrasal Verbs

- set up — organize or establish.
- come up with — think of an idea.
- read out — read aloud.

## Conversation 2 — Improving

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: I'm trying to cut down on tragic endings.
Diana: Good idea.
Anne: Miss Stacy says we should build on real experience.

### Useful Expressions

- cut down on — reduce.
- build on — develop from an existing base.
