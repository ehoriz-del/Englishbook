# Chapter 37 — The Reaper Whose Name Is Death

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Soon after Anne returns, tragedy strikes. Matthew learns that the bank where he and Marilla kept their savings has failed. The shock is too much for his weak heart.

He collapses at the doorway of Green Gables and dies. Anne is stunned. At first, her grief is so deep that she cannot cry. Matthew was the first person at Green Gables to accept her completely, and his quiet love always made her feel safe.

Later, Anne remembers his loving words and finally cries. After the funeral, life slowly begins moving again. Anne feels guilty when she notices beauty or experiences happiness, but Mrs. Allan helps her understand that continuing to live does not mean she loved Matthew less.

Anne and Marilla become closer in their shared grief. Matthew's death marks the end of Anne's childhood in a powerful way, and the future she imagined can no longer continue unchanged.

## Conversation 1 — The Loss

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: I don't know how we'll get through this.
Marilla: We'll have to lean on each other.
Anne: I keep expecting Matthew to walk through the door.

### Phrasal Verbs

- get through — survive a difficult period.
- lean on — depend on someone for emotional support.

## Conversation 2 — Moving Forward

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: It feels strange to carry on.
Mrs. Allan: You don't have to shut out every happy moment.
Anne: I'll always hold on to Matthew's memory.

### Useful Expressions

- carry on — continue despite difficulty.
- shut out — prevent something or someone from entering your life or thoughts.
- hold on to — keep something emotionally or physically.
