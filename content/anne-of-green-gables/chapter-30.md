# Chapter 30 — The Queen's Class Is Organized

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Miss Stacy organizes a special class for students who want to take the entrance examination for Queen's Academy. Anne joins along with Gilbert and several other Avonlea students. The work is demanding. Anne studies seriously because success at Queen's can lead to a teaching license and further education.

Her rivalry with Gilbert continues, but it has changed. They still compete for the highest marks, yet Anne increasingly respects his intelligence. Diana does not plan to attend Queen's. Anne is sad because she realizes their lives may soon move in different directions.

Anne is also changing. She is taller, quieter, and less likely to speak every thought aloud. Studying for Queen's gives her a new kind of dream: a goal that requires discipline and hard work.

## Conversation 1 — The New Class

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Miss Stacy: I'm putting together a Queen's class.
Anne: I'd like to sign up.
Miss Stacy: You'll have to keep up with extra work.

### Phrasal Verbs

- put together — organize or create.
- sign up — register.
- keep up with — stay at the required pace.

## Conversation 2 — Different Paths

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: I wish you were going on to Queen's.
Diana: It isn't my path.
Anne: I hope we don't drift apart.

### Useful Expressions

- go on to — continue to a next stage.
- drift apart — gradually become less close.
