# Chapter 28 — An Unfortunate Lily Maid

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Anne, Diana, Jane, and Ruby act out a romantic scene from Tennyson. Anne lies in a small boat and floats down the pond as if she were the dead heroine Elaine. At first, Anne enjoys the drama. Then she discovers that the boat is leaking.

It begins to sink. Anne manages to grab a bridge support and hangs there while the other girls run for help. Gilbert Blythe arrives in a boat and rescues her. He again apologizes for teasing her years earlier and asks if they can be friends.

For a moment, Anne considers forgiving him. But her pride returns, and she refuses. Gilbert becomes angry and leaves. Anne is safe but embarrassed by another adventure created by her love of romance.

Her feelings toward Gilbert are becoming more complicated, although she still refuses to admit it.

## Conversation 1 — The Boat

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: The boat is taking on water!
Diana: Hold on! We'll get help!

### Phrasal Verbs

- take on water — begin filling with water.
- hold on — keep holding; wait.
- get help — find someone who can assist.

## Conversation 2 — Gilbert's Offer

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Gilbert: Can't we put the past behind us?
Anne: I don't know.
Gilbert: I've tried to make it up to you.
Anne: I'm not ready to let it go.

### Useful Expressions

- put the past behind you — stop allowing past events to affect you.
- make it up to someone — compensate for a wrong.
- let it go — stop being angry about something.
