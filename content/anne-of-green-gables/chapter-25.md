# Chapter 25 — Matthew Insists on Puffed Sleeves

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Matthew notices that Anne's clothes are much plainer than those of her friends. He realizes that Anne especially wants a dress with fashionable puffed sleeves. Because Matthew is extremely shy around women, shopping for a dress is difficult.

He goes to a store but becomes nervous and buys several things he did not intend to buy. Eventually, he asks Mrs. Rachel Lynde for help. She offers to make Anne a beautiful brown dress with puffed sleeves.

On Christmas morning, Matthew gives the dress to Anne. She is overwhelmed with happiness. Even Marilla can see how much the gift means to her. The Christmas concert is a success, and Anne enjoys performing in her new dress.

Matthew's gift shows that although he says little, he notices what matters to Anne.

## Conversation 1 — Asking for Help

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Matthew: I want to pick out a dress for Anne.
Mrs. Lynde: You?
Matthew: I tried, but I got nervous.
Mrs. Lynde: I'll help you out.

### Phrasal Verbs

- pick out — choose.
- get nervous — begin to feel anxious.
- help out — give practical assistance.

## Conversation 2 — Christmas Morning

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: I can't get over these sleeves!
Matthew: Do you like them?
Anne: I'll take good care of this dress forever.

### Useful Expressions

- get over — stop being surprised or excited.
- take good care of — protect and keep in good condition.
