# Chapter 23 — Anne Comes to Grief in an Affair of Honor

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Anne's friends sometimes dare one another to prove their courage. One day, Josie Pye challenges Anne to walk along the ridgepole of a kitchen roof. Anne does not want to do it, but she is proud and does not want the others to think she is afraid.

She climbs onto the roof. Anne loses her balance and falls. She survives but breaks her ankle and must spend several weeks recovering. Marilla is frightened and angry that Anne took such a foolish risk because of a dare.

While recovering, Anne realizes that trying to prove courage is not the same as showing real courage. The accident becomes another lesson about pride and impulsive decisions.

## Conversation 1 — The Dare

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Josie: Are you going to back down?
Anne: No. I'll go through with it.
Diana: Don't give in to a foolish dare.

### Phrasal Verbs

- back down — stop accepting a challenge.
- go through with — do something despite difficulty.
- give in to — allow pressure to control you.

## Conversation 2 — Recovering

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: I should have thought it through.
Marilla: I hope you learned your lesson.
Anne: It'll take time to get back on my feet.

### Useful Expressions

- think through — consider the possible results carefully.
- get back on your feet — recover after illness, injury, or difficulty.
