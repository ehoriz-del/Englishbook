# Chapter 22 — Anne Is Invited Out to Tea

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Mrs. Allan invites Anne to tea at the manse. Anne is extremely excited because she admires Mrs. Allan and considers the invitation a great honor. Before going, Anne worries about how she should behave.

Marilla reminds her to use good manners and not talk too much. At the manse, Mrs. Allan makes Anne feel comfortable. They talk naturally, and Anne discovers that visiting an adult can be enjoyable rather than frightening.

Mrs. Allan treats Anne with respect and genuine interest. Anne returns home feeling that the afternoon was one of the happiest of her life. The visit is important because Anne is forming relationships with adults outside Green Gables.

Mrs. Allan becomes a positive influence who understands Anne's imagination.

## Conversation 1 — Before Tea

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: What if I say the wrong thing?
Marilla: Don't work yourself up.
Anne: I'll try not to go on and on.

### Phrasal Verbs

- work yourself up — make yourself increasingly worried.
- go on and on — talk for a long time.

## Conversation 2 — After the Visit

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Marilla: How did you get on?
Anne: Mrs. Allan made me feel at home.
Marilla: You weren't nervous?
Anne: Not after we got talking.

### Useful Expressions

- get on — manage or progress.
- feel at home — feel comfortable.
- get talking — begin a conversation.
