# Chapter 6 — Marilla Makes Up Her Mind

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Marilla and Anne arrive at Mrs. Spencer’s house. Marilla explains that there has been a mistake: she and Matthew asked for a boy, but Anne was sent instead. Mrs. Spencer is surprised and embarrassed, but she quickly suggests a solution.

Mrs. Blewett is looking for a girl to help with her children and household work, so Anne could live with her. Then Mrs. Blewett arrives. Marilla knows she has a reputation for being strict and difficult. Mrs. Blewett looks at Anne mainly as someone who can work for her.

Anne immediately becomes frightened. Marilla realizes that she cannot simply hand the girl over to someone she does not trust. Marilla tells Mrs. Spencer that she will take Anne back to Green Gables while she and Matthew think about the situation. When they return home, Matthew makes it clear that he wants Anne to stay.

Marilla finally agrees. Anne can remain at Green Gables. Marilla also makes one thing clear: if Anne is going to stay, she must be properly raised, educated, and taught how to behave. Anne is finally going to have a home.

### Phrasal Verbs

- take someone in — provide someone with a place to live.
- help out — give practical help.
- think something over — consider something carefully.

## Conversation 2 — The Decision

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Matthew: What are we going to do?
Marilla: I’ve made up my mind. She can stay.
Matthew: I’m glad you’ve come around.
Marilla: Don’t look so pleased.
Matthew: I knew you’d warm up to her.

### Useful Expressions

- make up your mind — make a decision.
- come around — change your opinion and begin to agree.
- warm up to someone — gradually begin to like someone.
