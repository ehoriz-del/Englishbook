# Chapter 3 — Marilla Cuthbert Is Surprised

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

When Matthew and Anne arrive at Green Gables, Marilla comes to meet them and is shocked. She was expecting a boy, but Matthew has brought home a red-haired girl. Matthew explains that Anne was waiting at the station and that there had clearly been a mistake.

Anne quickly understands the situation. She realizes that Matthew and Marilla did not want her at all. They wanted a boy who could help with the farm. Anne is heartbroken. She begins to cry and says that this is the worst thing that has ever happened to her.

She had been so excited about finally having a home, and now she believes she will have to return to the orphanage. Marilla thinks Anne is dramatic, but she also begins to feel sorry for her. Anne says she wishes her name were something beautiful such as Cordelia.

If Marilla must call her Anne, she asks her to spell it with an “e.” Marilla tells Anne that she can stay at Green Gables for the night. They will decide what to do the next day. Meanwhile, Matthew quietly tells Marilla that perhaps they could keep Anne.

Marilla is surprised again. They had planned to adopt a boy for a practical reason, but Matthew has already become fond of Anne.

### Phrasal Verbs

- show up — arrive.
- mix something up — confuse one thing with another.
- find out — discover information.

## Conversation 2 — What Should We Do?

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Matthew: Maybe we could keep her around.
Marilla: We agreed to get a boy.
Matthew: Why don’t we think it over?
Marilla: We can’t simply change our minds tonight.

### Useful Expressions

- keep someone around — allow someone to remain with you.
- think something over — consider something carefully.
- change your mind — decide something different.
