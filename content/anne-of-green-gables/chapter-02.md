# Chapter 2 — Matthew Cuthbert Is Surprised

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Matthew Cuthbert drives to the train station to pick up the orphan boy he and Marilla are expecting. When he arrives, however, there is no boy waiting for him. Instead, Matthew sees a thin, red-haired girl sitting at the station with a small bag beside her.

Her name is Anne Shirley. The stationmaster explains that Anne is the child who was sent from the orphanage. Matthew realizes that there has been a mistake. Matthew is extremely shy, especially around women and girls.

He does not know how to explain the problem to Anne, so he decides to take her home and let Marilla deal with the situation. Anne is delighted to see him. As soon as they leave the station, she begins talking, and she hardly stops. Anne tells Matthew about herself, her imagination, and the things she loves.

She is especially excited by the beautiful countryside around them. As they travel toward Green Gables, Anne gives imaginative names to the places they pass, including the “White Way of Delight” and the “Lake of Shining Waters.” Matthew is not used to talking very much, but he enjoys listening to Anne. Her excitement and imagination make the quiet journey much more interesting.

Anne tells him that she has always dreamed of having a real home. Matthew begins to feel uncomfortable because he knows Marilla wanted a boy, not a girl. Anne is so happy that he cannot bring himself to tell her the truth.

Finally, they arrive at Green Gables. Matthew knows that Marilla is about to discover that something has gone very wrong.

### Phrasal Verbs

- pick someone up — go somewhere to collect someone.
- show up — arrive or appear somewhere.
- sort something out — solve a problem or confusing situation.
