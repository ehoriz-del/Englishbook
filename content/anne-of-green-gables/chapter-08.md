# Chapter 8 — Anne's Bringing-Up Is Begun

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

The next morning, Marilla tells Anne that she and Matthew have decided to keep her at Green Gables. Anne is so happy that she can hardly believe it. She wants to hug Marilla, but Marilla is not comfortable showing affection so openly.

Marilla explains that Anne can stay if she tries to behave properly and obey the rules. Anne promises to do her best. She is eager to belong somewhere and to become the kind of girl Marilla wants her to be. Anne soon asks about other children nearby.

Marilla tells her about Diana Barry, a girl who lives close by. Anne immediately hopes Diana will become the close friend she has always dreamed of having. Matthew is delighted that Anne is staying.

Unlike Marilla, he does not worry much about correcting her. He simply enjoys having her around. Green Gables is already changing. The quiet house now has Anne's questions, stories, excitement, and mistakes in it.

Marilla realizes that bringing Anne up will not be simple, but she has accepted the responsibility.

## Conversation 1 — You Can Stay

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Marilla: We've decided you can stay.
Anne: Really? You're not going to send me away?
Marilla: No, but you'll have to follow through on your
promises.
Anne: I will. I'll do my best.

### Phrasal Verbs

- send someone away — make someone leave. They decided not to send him away.
- follow through on — do what you promised. You need to follow through on your commitment.

## Conversation 2 — A Possible Friend

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: Do you think Diana and I will get along?
Marilla: You'll have to meet her and find out.
Anne: I hope we hit it off right away.
Marilla: Try to calm down until you actually meet her.

### Useful Expressions

- get along — have a friendly relationship. I get along with my neighbors.
- find out — discover information. I'll call and find out.
- hit it off — quickly become friendly. We hit it off immediately.
- calm down — become less excited or upset. Take a minute to calm down.
