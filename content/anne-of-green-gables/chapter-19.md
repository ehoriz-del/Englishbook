# Chapter 19 — A Concert, a Catastrophe, and a Confession

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Anne and Diana attend a concert and later stay at Diana's house while Mr. and Mrs. Barry are away. For fun, the girls race into the spare room and jump onto the bed. Unfortunately, Diana's elderly Aunt Josephine has arrived unexpectedly and is already sleeping there.

Aunt Josephine is furious and threatens to leave. Diana worries because her aunt has always been generous to the family. Anne feels responsible and apologizes sincerely.

Instead of making a dramatic performance, she honestly explains what happened. Aunt Josephine is amused by Anne's personality and forgives the girls. The accident creates a new friendship, and Anne's sincere apology shows that she is becoming more mature.

## Conversation 1 — A Terrible Surprise

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: We really messed up.
Diana: She wants to leave tomorrow.
Anne: I'll own up to what we did.

### Phrasal Verbs

- mess up — make a mistake.
- own up to — admit responsibility.

## Conversation 2 — The Apology

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: I hope you can forgive us.
Aunt Josephine: At least you came forward and apologized.
Anne: Can we put it behind us?
Aunt Josephine: Yes.

### Useful Expressions

- come forward — present yourself to give information or accept responsibility.
- put something behind you — stop focusing on a past problem.

- wake someone up — cause someone who is sleeping to become awake. Please don’t wake the baby up.
