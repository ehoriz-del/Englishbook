# Chapter 27 — Vanity and Vexation of Spirit

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Anne still dislikes her red hair. A traveling salesman offers her a dye that he promises will turn it black. Anne secretly buys the dye and uses it. Instead of black, her hair turns a terrible green. She is horrified. Marilla eventually discovers what happened.

The color cannot easily be removed, so Anne's hair has to be cut very short. Anne admits that vanity caused the mistake. She wanted so badly to change herself that she trusted a stranger without thinking carefully.

As her hair grows back, Anne becomes less obsessed with its color. The disaster teaches her to accept herself more than before.

## Conversation 1 — The Dye

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Marilla: What have you done to your hair?
Anne: I tried to get rid of the red.
Marilla: And it turned into green?

### Phrasal Verbs

- get rid of — remove something unwanted.
- turn into — change into something different.

## Conversation 2 — A Lesson

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: I'll never fall for that kind of promise again.
Marilla: Good.
Anne: I was too caught up in wanting black hair.
Marilla: Maybe you'll grow out of worrying about it.

### Useful Expressions

- fall for — believe a trick.
- be caught up in — be deeply focused on something.
- grow out of — stop a habit or interest as you mature.
