# Chapter 33 — The Hotel Concert

## Story Summary — A2–B1 Level

Anne is invited to perform a recitation at a fashionable hotel at White Sands. Diana helps her choose what to wear, and Anne wears the pearl necklace Matthew gave her. When Anne sees elegant women in expensive clothes and jewelry, she suddenly feels plain and out of place.

On stage, her nervousness becomes worse. She is tempted to give up. Then she sees Gilbert in the audience, and her pride gives her courage. Anne performs beautifully and receives strong applause.

Afterward, she realizes that wealthy, elegant people are simply human beings. Their clothes do not make them more important. The experience strengthens Anne's confidence.

She learns to focus on what she can do instead of comparing herself with others.

## Conversation 1 — Before the Performance

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Anne: I don't fit in here.
Diana: Don't compare yourself to them.
Anne: I need to pull myself together.

### Phrasal Verbs

- fit in — feel accepted in a group.
- compare yourself to — judge yourself against someone else.
- pull yourself together — calm yourself and regain control.

## Conversation 2 — Afterward

*A modern conversation inspired by this chapter.*

Diana: You pulled it off!
Anne: I almost ran away.
Diana: But you went through with it.
Anne: The fear wore off once I started.

### Useful Expressions

- pull it off — succeed at something difficult.
- go through with — complete something despite difficulty.
- wear off — gradually disappear.
